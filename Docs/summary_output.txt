forecasts in the English Premier League betting market. In general, the paper finds that statistical

over a long sample period. While it is interesting to see the relative performance of the models considered
here (some well-known and implementable models such as xGBoost and randomforest, the results are not
overly surprising.

I enjoyed reading this paper; the question is interesting, and the overall methodology is sound. However,

First, the claim that the market fundamentally changed around 2015 is central, but it is currently

statistics. This is an important finding and should be discussed more directly, since it affects how we
interpret the results and what the study is truly testing.

League betting market. The main conclusion is interesting: even when the models achieve reasonable

improves transparency and reproducibility.
